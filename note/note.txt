Ipadeola Sunday Abidemi  is a talking drummer Born in Nigeria, he is professionally known for playing talking drum. He started his journey in drumming professionally in 2012, at The Redeemed Christian Church of God Lagos. This was as a result of his love in drumming and singing. Ipadeola hails from Oyo town, a city known for home of culture and traditions.

I studied Marketing at Lagos State Polytechnic and graduated  in 2017 and also a member of Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria, after I moved to the UK, I joined another Redeemed Christian Church of God where I currently play  taking drum as a professional.

Ipadeola, is not just playing taking drum but entertaining people both old and young. Each time I play the talking drum, I see how people feel relaxed and happy. Sometimes, people don’t want me to go and that is the extent that they enjoy the drum.

As a professional talking drummer, I can use the drum to send messages or make a proverbs. Sometimes, a talking drum can be used to praise warn someone of a danger ahead.

Talking drum is mainly used among the Yorubas which is the western part of Nigeria.
