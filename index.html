ABOUT THE ARTIST

The Rhythm of Heritage, Devotion & Pure Joy

Ipadeola Sunday Abidemi, celebrated across circles as Ipadeola D Drummer, is a Nigerian-born virtuoso talking drummer whose sonic storytelling breathes vitality, joy, and deep cultural resonance wherever his rhythms land.

Ipadeola hails from Oyo town, an ancient city universally revered as the historic home of Yoruba royalty, culture, and drumming traditions. Growing up immersed in this ancestral environment, drumming was not merely an art form to Sunday—it was a native mother tongue.

In 2012, he officially started his professional journey in drumming at The Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG) Lagos. This arose directly from his heartfelt passion for singing and drumming, channeling sacred rhythms into spirit-filled praise.

“Ipadeola is not just playing the talking drum, but entertaining people both old and young. Each time I play the talking drum, I see how people feel relaxed and happy. Sometimes, people don’t want me to go—and that is the extent that they enjoy the drum.”

Beyond his artistic calling, Sunday is an accomplished scholar and professional. He studied Marketing at Lagos State Polytechnic (LASPOTECH), graduating in 2017, and is also an accredited member of the Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria (CIBN). This unique union of professional marketing acumen, disciplined financial intellect, and authentic cultural mastery shapes his high-level professionalism and stage craft.

Following his relocation to the United Kingdom, Sunday made his home in Doncaster, where he joined RCCG in the UK. Today, he regularly ministers, collaborates with musicians, performs at luxury weddings and cultural galas, and hosts cultural percussion masterclasses for youth and community groups.

TRADITIONAL YORUBA PERCUSSION

The Language of the Talking Drum

In Yoruba tradition, the talking drum is an acoustic linguistic marvel. Squeezing the leather tension strings mimics the tonal highs, lows, and glides of human speech—enabling the drummer to speak poetry, recite ancestral proverbs, praise dignitaries, and warn of danger ahead.

01 Lead • The Mother

Ìyá Ìlù

Mother’s Drum

The grand, deep-voiced matriarch of the ensemble. Decorated with small brass bells (saworo), the Ìyá Ìlù commands the conversation, initiates complex proverbs, directs the dancers, and delivers royal praises.

Deep Resonant Tone Proverb Articulation
02 The Vocal Master

Gángan

Talking Drum

The iconic hourglass talking drum held under the armpit and struck with the curved Ọpá Gángan stick. By squeezing the leather cords, Sunday modulates its pitch across several octaves to mimic speech with uncanny accuracy.

Dynamic Pitch Shifting Spoken Tones (Dò-Re-Mí)
03 Harmonic Anchor

Ọmẹlẹ Gángan

Accompaniment Drum

A smaller talking drum tuned to a higher, tight pitch that locks the groove. It provides a relentless, infectious rhythmic foundation over which the lead drum speaks and improvises.

Soprano Pitch Polyrhythmic Groove
04 Sacred Fire

Ọmẹlẹ Batá

Batá Complementary Drum

Part of the ancient conical Batá drum family associated with royal courts and venerated ceremonies. Produces sharp, snappy, and percussive syncopations that inject electrifying intensity.

Conical Shape Sharp Syncopation
05 Earthy Tradition

Sakara / Cord

Classic Clay Frame Drum

Crafted from a baked clay circular ring and stretched goatskin held with cord pegs. Played with a light wooden stick, it evokes classic philosophical contemplation, traditional weddings, and deep Yoruba acoustic folklore.

Clay Ring Resonator Acoustic Folklore

Traditional Yoruba Proverbs & Messages Delivered Through The Drum:

CHRONOLOGY & MILESTONES

The Artistic Journey

From childhood roots in cultural Oyo town to Lagos polytechnic halls and United Kingdom sanctuaries.

  1. Roots
    Cultural Heritage

    Born in Oyo Town, Nigeria

    Raised in the heartland of Yoruba civilization, renowned worldwide as the cultural epicenter of talking drumming, royal ceremonies, and folklore traditions. Sunday absorbed rhythmic mastery naturally from ancestral artisans.

  2. 2012
    Ministry & Career Debut

    Professional Journey at RCCG Lagos

    Sparked by his burning passion for singing and drumming, Sunday commenced his professional drumming career at The Redeemed Christian Church of God in Lagos. He quickly became renowned for uplifting church congregations and bringing relaxation and joy to audiences.

  3. 2017
    Academic & Professional Distinction

    Marketing Graduate & Banker (CIBN)

    Graduated with honors in Marketing from Lagos State Polytechnic (LASPOTECH). Certified as an associate member of the Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria (CIBN), establishing a reputation for discipline, strategic communication, and high professional standards.

  4. UK
    International Expansion

    Settling in Doncaster & RCCG UK

    Relocated to the United Kingdom, establishing his artistic residence in Doncaster, South Yorkshire. Joined RCCG in the UK where he continues his sacred ministry as resident professional talking drummer while serving the wider UK diaspora.

  5. Today
    Cultural Ambassador

    Stage Performances, Weddings & Masterclasses

    Entertaining audiences young and old across Great Britain. Captivating wedding receptions, cultural festivals, and directing youth workshops where children discover the historic marvel of African rhythm.
